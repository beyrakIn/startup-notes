# The Four Pillars of a Great Startup

> Source: Sam Altman — "How to Start a Startup" (Stanford CS183B, Lecture 1)
> Captured: 2026-05-31

Sam Altman frames a great startup as four things that must *all* be hard to get right. You need a great idea, a great product, a great team, and great execution — and even then, lots of luck.

| # | Pillar | The core idea |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **Idea** | Start with a mission-driven idea you'd work on even if it stays small. Easier to recruit and stay motivated. Counter-intuitively, the best ideas often sound bad at first (so they have little competition) but are actually good. |
| 2 | **Product** | Build something a small number of users *love*, not something a lot of users kind of like. The job is a great product + a great relationship with your early users. Talk to users, watch them use it, iterate. |
| 3 | **Team** | A great team is the only way to build a great company. Hire slowly, only people you'd be thrilled to work with. Co-founder relationships matter enormously — most startup deaths trace back to founder conflict. |
| 4 | **Execution** | Ideas are worthless without execution. The founder sets the standard; the company becomes what the founders do, not what they say. Two things to track relentlessly: **growth** and **momentum**. |

## Key takeaways

- **All four are necessary.** A great idea with bad execution is worthless; great execution on a bad idea fails too.
- **Love > Like.** Better to have 100 users who love you than 10,000 who are indifferent. Love is what spreads by word of mouth.
- **The idea matters more than people think.** The "ideas don't matter, only execution does" advice is overstated — a hard-to-copy, long-term idea is what compounds.
- **Founders are the standard-setters.** Everything — quality, intensity, focus — flows down from how the founders themselves operate.

## Questions to ask myself about my startup

- [ ] **Idea:** Can I explain it in one clear sentence? Is it a mission I'd commit years to? Why now?
- [ ] **Product:** Do I have even a handful of users who would be genuinely upset if it disappeared?
- [ ] **Team:** Would I be excited to spend the next 10 years with my co-founder(s)?
- [ ] **Execution:** What is my single growth metric, and is it moving every week?
